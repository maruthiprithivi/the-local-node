#   next begins. The files are 1248x702 at 24 fps, starting at 0 (ffprobe).
# - ffmpeg (linuxserver/ffmpeg) wrote the counter's two digits (crop 34x24 at 1131,27, gray) of every
#   frame; a script (python:3.13-slim) took the mean absolute pixel change between frames. Each video
#   has exactly (scenes - 1) changes of 3.7 or more; every other frame changes by 0.83 or less.
# - A scene starts on the first frame with the new counter (frame k, at k / 24 s). The time is rounded
#   UP to one decimal, so a player seeking to it always lands inside the new scene (each scene's audio
#   starts with a 0.35 s lead-in, so nothing spoken is skipped).
# - Checks: all 18 videos: the frame shown at each time has the same counter as the middle of its own
#   scene and a different one from the scene before, and the count matches narration.json. Frames at
#   every time were grabbed with the counter enlarged; for 01, 02, 06, 09, 15 and 17 every frame was
#   looked at: the counter reads scene k+1 and the burned-in subtitle is the first words of scene k's
#   narration.
